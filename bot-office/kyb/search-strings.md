# Search strings for Analyst to run items

**Source:** SOP Business Adverse Media, Sanctions and PEP Review (28 Oct 2026); Part B s5
**Last updated:** 29 October 2026
Local-language terms are search aids drafted for analysts. Ask a native speaker to check before relying on a no-result search.

## Core English library (run for every subject and name variant)
- "<name>" AND (fraud OR scam OR "money laundering" OR laundering OR embezzlement)
- "<name>" AND (bribery OR corruption OR kickback OR "tax evasion")
- "<name>" AND (sanction OR sanctions OR OFAC OR terrorist OR "terrorism financing")
- "<name>" AND (arrest OR charged OR convicted OR indicted OR lawsuit OR investigation)
- "<name>" AND (regulator OR fine OR penalty OR "cease and desist" OR "license revoked")
- "<name>" AND (minister OR mayor OR governor OR senator OR congressman OR ambassador OR judge)

## Gaming merchants (Part B s5)
- "<brand or entity>" AND (licence OR license) AND (fake OR revoked OR suspended OR "not recognised")
- "<regulator name>" AND (fictitious OR illegal OR warning OR unlicensed)
- "<entity>" AND ("withdrawal" OR "non-payment" OR complaint OR scam)
- "<UBO name>" AND (casino OR gambling) AND (arrest OR fraud OR investigation OR "money laundering")
- "<domain>" AND (blacklist OR unlicensed OR mirror)

## Forex merchants
- "<entity or brand>" site:iosco.org (I-SCAN) and the regulator warning list in each target market
- "<entity or brand>" AND (unauthorised OR unauthorized OR "clone firm" OR "warning list")
